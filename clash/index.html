CONTROLS

KEYBOARD P1: A/D move · W jump · S crouch/block · J light punch · K heavy kick · L special · I throw

KEYBOARD P2: ←/→ move · ↑ jump · ↓ block · numpad 1 punch · numpad 2 kick · numpad 3 special · numpad 0 throw

GAMEPAD: left stick move · A jump · B block · X punch · Y kick · RB special · LB throw

TOUCH: virtual stick + on-screen buttons (auto-shown on mobile)

Combos: Each fighter has unique combos shown on their detail page in FIGHTERS. Press the indicated keys in sequence within ~0.4s of each other.

SPECIAL METER: Builds as you land hits and take hits. When full, the SPECIAL key triggers your unique super — each fighter destroys the opponent in a different way.
