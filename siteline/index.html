Siteline — Free 5v5 Counter-Strike Style FPS in Your Browser

Siteline is a free, no-download, no-install browser FPS. It runs entirely in your web browser using WebGL — no app stores, no executables, no signups, no email. Open the page, click a button, and you are inside a 5v5 bombsite shooter in under five seconds.

Multiplayer with a room code — up to 50 players

To play with friends, click HOST to generate a unique six-character room code. Share that code with anyone — Discord, text, voice, paste it anywhere. Your friends click JOIN, paste the code, and they are in. A single Siteline room supports up to 50 simultaneous players. Connections are peer-to-peer over WebRTC, so there is no central server to wait for. If a friend cannot connect, empty slots are filled with CPU bots at your chosen difficulty.

Three classic-style maps: office, dust, and dust 2

Siteline ships with three handcrafted maps designed in the Counter-Strike tradition. The office is a tight indoor map with cubicles, server racks, and fluorescent lighting. Dust is a sun-drenched desert village with mudbrick walls and tower cover. Dust 2 is the larger two-bombsite map every CS player remembers — long sightlines, mid catwalks, A and B sites at opposite corners. Each map has dynamic point lights, soft shadows, sun lighting, fog, and bombsite markers.

Three weapons and three grenades

Your loadout includes a pistol (handgun), the AK-47 assault rifle, and a bullpup rifle — each with its own damage, recoil, fire rate, and spread. Grenades include the classic HE grenade (high-explosive area damage), the flashbang (blinds enemies who look at it), and the smoke grenade (renders a visual smoke cloud that blocks line-of-sight for sixteen seconds).

Solo mode with three bot difficulties

If you do not have friends online, play solo against CPU terrorists or counter-terrorists. Choose easy, medium, or hard bots. Hard bots react in 0.14 seconds, easy bots wander the map and miss. Team sizes are configurable: 3v3, 5v5, or 8v8 in solo, and up to 25v25 in multiplayer.

Counter-Strike inspired economy and round system

Every round starts with a 15-second buy menu phase. Earn money by winning rounds and getting kills. Spend it on weapons, kevlar armor, and grenades. First team to win 8 rounds takes the match. The classic 5v5 bombsite format is preserved: CT and T spawns at opposite ends, A and B bombsites marked on the ground, win by eliminating the other team.

Desktop and mobile — same game, full controls

On desktop you get WASD movement, mouse aim, click to fire, R to reload, 1 and 2 to swap weapons, G to cycle grenades, F to throw, B for buy menu, TAB for scoreboard, ENTER for chat, SPACE to jump, SHIFT to walk silently. On mobile, full touch controls — virtual movement and look sticks, plus dedicated FIRE, RELOAD, SWAP, NADE, and BUY buttons.

What is Siteline?

Siteline is a tribute to and small reimagining of competitive tactical shooters like Counter-Strike (CS), CS:GO, CS2, Valorant, Rainbow Six Siege, and the long lineage of 5v5 round-based first-person shooters. It is not affiliated with any of these. It is a single-page HTML5 game built with Three.js for rendering and PeerJS for multiplayer signaling. Source is open in your browser — view source any time. Free to play, free to host, free to share. No ads in-game. The dev accepts tips via the SUPPORT THE DEV button.

Why play in a browser?

No download means no antivirus warnings, no install size, no patches, no account. Click a link and play. Send a friend a link and they play. The game is small enough to load on a hotel wi-fi connection. It works on a Chromebook, on an old laptop, on a phone, on a tablet. The renderer auto-scales — devicePixelRatio is capped, draw distance fits within mobile GPU budgets, shadow maps are conservative.
