— THE LAST CARTOGRAPHER —
The fire came through in the autumn, and the valley you grew up in was a black smear on the map by morning.

You went away. You drew other places, for other people. Twenty-three years passed and you did not come back.

Now you have come back. You carry your grandfather's lantern, a staff with a striking-iron at the tip, and an empty book. Walk every stone, your grandmother had said, when you were small. Walk every stone, and the valley remembers itself.
