— SIX STONES. ONE FLAME. —
On the night the year turns, the grove behind your village fills with waystones. Six of them, set into the trees by hands older than anyone living. Each one belongs to somebody who is gone.

Your grandmother gave you the lantern an hour ago. Four wicks of oil, she said. Six stones. You'll have to choose.

Walk the grove. Light what you mean to light. Carry the rest home.
Move: WASD or arrows · Interact: E or space
On a phone: joystick + button

A short walk in the dark. ~10 minutes. Your choices stay with the stones.
