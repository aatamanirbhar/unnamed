Blacksite — Free CIA Stealth Infiltration in Your Browser

Blacksite is a free top-down stealth infiltration game that runs in any modern web browser. You play a CIA ghost inside a hostile facility. Steal the intel. Reach the exit. Never be seen. Free, no download, no install, no signup.

Tranq pistol, takedowns, distraction coins

The core kit is built around non-lethal stealth: a silenced tranquilizer dart pistol that drops a guard from across a room in one shot, silent takedowns when you approach from behind, and distraction coins that lure guards out of their patrol routes. Every option starts limited — three darts, two coins, one hack charge — and you pick a perk between every floor to extend your kit.

Security cameras and hackable terminals

Mounted cameras sweep their vision cones across rooms and corridors. If a camera catches you, every guard within 500 units gets your last known position. Security terminals are scattered through every floor — walk up to one, press E, and you trip the building's security for several seconds: cameras go dark, guards lose their vision cones, you walk through the corridor unseen. Pick the LONGER HACK perk to extend the window.

Bodies stay where they fell

An unconscious guard is a clue. If another guard sees a body in their cone, they spike to alert and walk over to investigate. Press E next to a body to hide it — it stays in place but goes from solid to a faint outline, no longer triggering patrols. Use this to clear a room: takedown, hide, takedown, hide.

Per-floor upgrade picks

Reaching the exit doesn't end the run — it opens a perk screen. Three random choices from: +2 DARTS, +2 COINS, +1 HACK CHARGE, LONGER HACK DURATION, LONGER ARM (wider takedown reach). The choice persists for the rest of the run.

Controls

Desktop: WASD / arrows for movement. Mouse to aim. Left click or F to fire a tranq dart at the cursor. Right click or Q to throw a distraction coin at the cursor. E for context-sensitive action (takedown / hide body / hack terminal). SHIFT to crouch silently. M to toggle minimap. Mobile: virtual movement stick, CROUCH toggle, side buttons for ACT, DART, COIN, and MAP.

Why play in a browser?

No download, no installer, no app store, no account. Open the page and play. Blacksite runs on Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS, ChromeOS. The game is small, loads on hotel wi-fi, plays on phones and Chromebooks.
