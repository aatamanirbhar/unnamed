Privacy, plainly

— Last updated 2026-06-09 —

Short version: Omenly is a small handmade arcade. It collects the bare minimum to keep working, and never sells anything about you.

What gets stored on your device

  • Honor passes. When you click "I donated — turn off ads," a token like omenly.pass.echo or omenly.pass.all is saved in your browser's localStorage. That token never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data removes it.

What we measure

  • Anonymous visit counts via Vercel Web Analytics — pageviews, country, device type. No IP addresses, no cookies, no fingerprinting. You can read Vercel's policy here.

Third parties

  • PayPal — only invoked when you click a donate link. PayPal handles its own data per its privacy policy. Omenly never sees your payment details.
  • Google AdSense — when ads are enabled, AdSense may use cookies to serve ads. You can opt out at Google Ads Settings. Clicking "I donated" hides ads on this device, which also stops AdSense from loading.

What we don't do

  • No accounts. No email collection. No newsletters.
  • No selling, sharing, or licensing your data to anyone.
  • No tracking across other sites.

Children

Some games on Omenly are 18+ (clearly marked). The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Contact

Reach out via the link on the about page. Take-down requests, privacy questions, or general notes — all welcome.
