ECHO — Upload to itch.io
========================

1. Zip the folder:
   - Right-click "echo" folder → Send to → Compressed (zipped) folder
   - Must contain index.html at the ROOT of the zip (not inside a subfolder)

2. Go to https://itch.io/game/new

3. Fill in:
   - Title: ECHO
   - Project URL: echo (or whatever)
   - Short description: A sonar survival game. You cannot see. Click to listen.
   - Kind of project: HTML
   - Upload your zip → check "This file will be played in the browser"
   - Viewport dimensions: leave blank, or set 1280x720 / "fullscreen"
   - Embed options: check "Fullscreen button" and "Mobile friendly"

4. Genre: Adventure / Action
   Tags: atmospheric, dark, exploration, minimalist, procedural-generation,
         singleplayer, sonar, top-down

5. Pricing: "No payment" (the in-game donate button handles tipping)

6. Visibility: Draft first → playtest in the itch.io player → then Public

The PayPal donate button inside the game points to:
   https://www.paypal.me/vikas117

A reality check:
- itch.io has ~hundreds of new games per day. Getting noticed needs a good cover
  image, a 15-second gif preview, and posting it places people will see it
  (r/WebGames, r/incremental_games, r/IndieDev, Twitter/Bluesky, TikTok clips).
- A polished free game with a donate button averages a few dollars unless it
  goes viral. Anything more requires marketing, not just code.
- The mechanic here (sonar/echolocation) is genuinely uncommon, which gives it
  a real shot at being shared. Make a 10-second clip of a ping revealing an
  enemy right next to the player — that's your hook.
