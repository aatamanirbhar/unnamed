Echo — Free Sonar Survival Horror in Your Browser

Echo is a free top-down sonar survival horror game playable in any modern browser. There are no lights. You cannot see the world. Each ping you fire expands outward like sonar, revealing tiles and enemies for a brief moment before everything fades back into pitch dark. Your battery is shared between pings, your flashlight, and your EMP — every action drains it. Find battery cells. Find the exit. Descend.

Five enemy archetypes

Echo's roster of monsters is built around sound mechanics. Each one teaches you a different way the dark can kill you.

  • STALKER — Slow. Silent. Always closing. Slows when your ping lands directly on it.
  • HOWLER — Emits its own pings on a loud cycle. Hard to miss. Brings the room with it.
  • HUNTER — Hears every ping you fire. Sprints to the spot you pinged from.
  • TANK — Massive HP. Charges in a straight line when it gets line of sight.
  • WHISPERER — Almost invisible. Only briefly visible when pinged or flashlit. Dashes in bursts.

Mechanics

The core tools are PING, FLASHLIGHT, and EMP. Ping reveals tiles within line of sight in an expanding ring. Flashlight lights a wide forward cone and stuns Whisperers. EMP stuns every enemy in radius for several seconds and reveals the surrounding tiles. All three share a single battery meter. Battery cells respawn in each procedural floor. Crouch with SHIFT to move silently — Hunters cannot hear your footsteps when crouched.

Levels and progression

Each floor is a procedurally generated maze with rooms and an exit hatch. The exit is always the farthest open tile from your spawn. The deeper you go, the more enemies, the wider the enemy pool, the smaller the battery cell count. Eight named depths in rotation: Shallows, Wet Corridor, Boneyard, Old Substation, Chapel, Channel, Engine Room, Abyss. Your heart rate visibly climbs as enemies get close, audible as a heartbeat thump in the audio mix.

Controls

Desktop: WASD to move, MOUSE for facing, LMB or click to ping, SHIFT to crouch silently, F for flashlight, X for EMP. Mobile: virtual stick for movement, large PING button, side buttons for FLASH, EMP, CROUCH.

Why play in a browser?

No download. No install. No antivirus warning. No account. Open the page and play. Echo is built with HTML5 Canvas and Web Audio — works on Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, on desktop, Android, iOS, Chromebook. Loads on hotel wi-fi. Plays on phones that struggle with Steam.
