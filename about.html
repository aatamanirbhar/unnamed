— OMENLY —

about the arcade

A little corner of the web for small, handmade games.

What this is

Omenly is a tiny browser arcade — short, finished games you can open in a tab and play through in five to thirty minutes. No installs. No accounts. No quests with daily login bonuses dragging you back.

Each game lives at its own address but shares the same little porch light: a footer, an honor system, and a promise that nothing here is trying to trick you.

The honor system

Every game has ads (someday — we're small, give us time). Every game also has a donate button with a suggested price and a second button that says "I donated — turn off ads." No verification. No DRM. If you click it, ads turn off forever on your browser. We trust you.

On the hub there's a single $10 all-access pass — it propagates to every game once you click through, and to future games too.

If something here stays with you, the support button is right there. If it doesn't, that's okay too — close the tab and we're square.

Who made this

Omenly is built and run by one person who likes small games and quiet aesthetics. Each title is handmade, frame by frame, line by line.

Contact

The fastest way to reach me is through my PayPal note field (it's silly but it works) or the address listed on the project's GitHub when it's public. For take-downs, business, or quiet praise — same channel.

Built with

Plain HTML, plain CSS, plain JavaScript. No frameworks. Hosted on Vercel. Analytics by Vercel Web Analytics (anonymous, no cookies). Payments through PayPal. Ads, eventually, through Google AdSense.
